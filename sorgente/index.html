Put it on your phone

iPhone and iPad

Install it as an app from Safari: full screen, its own icon, works offline.

  1. 1Open this page in Safari.
  2. 2Tap Share.
  3. 3Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

iOS does not let any app draw over the home screen or other apps, so on iPhone the game runs full screen.

Android: your whole phone is the ring

The app turns your real screens into the arena: the home screen, the lock screen, any app or web page. Fighters stand on icons and buttons while the phone stays usable.

Download the APK
  1. 1Open the downloaded file. Chrome may warn that the file could be harmful: it is the standard warning for apps from outside the Play Store. Tap Download anyway.
  2. 2Allow Install unknown apps for Chrome when Android asks, then Install.
  3. 3Android 13 or later: if the accessibility switch is grey, open App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings. The app reads only where elements are on screen, never their text.

Prefer not to install an APK? In Chrome use ⋮ → Add to Home screen for the full-screen game.

On a computer

Play here with the keyboard (S to parry, arrows to move), or put the fighters on every web page with the Chrome extension.

Chrome extension

No ads, no tracking. Settings stay on your device. Only the name you choose for the leaderboard and your score are sent, when you send them.
